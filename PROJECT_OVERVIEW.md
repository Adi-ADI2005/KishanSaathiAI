# 🌾 Kishan Saathi AI — Project Overview

## 🚜 Introduction

Kishan Saathi AI is a modern AI-powered agriculture platform designed to support farmers with intelligent digital solutions. The project focuses on solving real-world agricultural challenges using Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Weather APIs, and multilingual chatbot assistance.

The platform acts as a smart companion for farmers by providing crop recommendations, disease detection, weather insights, government scheme assistance, and AI-driven guidance.

---

# 🎯 Objective

The main objective of this project is:

- To help farmers make better agricultural decisions
- To improve crop productivity using AI
- To provide easy access to farming information
- To digitally empower rural communities
- To bridge the gap between technology and agriculture

---

# 🧠 Core Functionalities

## 🌱 Crop Recommendation
Predicts suitable crops based on:
- Soil data
- Temperature
- Humidity
- Rainfall

---

## 🦠 Disease Detection
Farmers can upload crop images and the system predicts possible diseases with prevention suggestions.

---

## 🌦 Weather Dashboard
Provides:
- Live weather updates
- Wind speed
- Humidity
- Sunrise & sunset
- Temperature conversion

---

## 🏛 Government Schemes
Helps farmers discover:
- Agriculture subsidies
- Insurance schemes
- Government support programs

---

## 🤖 AI Chatbot
Interactive multilingual assistant that answers farming-related questions.
